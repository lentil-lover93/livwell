Hi, I’m Liv 👋

Through years of personal and professional experience, I've found that regular exercise and mindfulness practices are instrumental to enhancing my overall physical and mental wellness. More so, I’ve come to the realization that my true purpose in life is to share this cultivated knowledge with others and assist them in becoming the healthiest and most authentic version of themselves. Alongside working with me, you’ll find you’re living your best and happiest life. This feels and looks like increased strength, stamina, and overall energy of your physical and mental being.

With Gratitude,

Liv Skinner, founder of Liv Well, featured in WIRED magazine for her work at the intersect of innovation and wellness

I’m incredibly honored to be featured in WIRED magazine alongside Airbnb, representing my work as a Bay Area wellness professional and personal trainer. This opportunity came through a networking connection, which reminded me just how important it is to stay open, genuine, and intentional with how you show up. You never know what kind of impact you're having on someone or what seeds you’re planting. Every interaction matters. It’s a full-circle moment I’ll always carry with gratitude. Proof that when you lead with purpose and integrity, it comes back to you— in all ways, always.

Here’s to creating impact, lifting others higher, and continuing to Liv Well together forever.
